Ananas is a land design studio working on regenerative land systems — masterplanning, site assessments, ongoing land stewardship, and hands-on implementation support, alongside workshops and educational walks. Based in Bangalore but working across climates, they describe their core purpose as co-creating "ecological systems" with land and people, adapting regenerative design principles to each site rather than applying a fixed template.

Their process is seasonally disciplined: earthworks run December–March, planting-pit preparation September–June, and actual planting May–August, with design and implementation treated as separate engagements planned 2–3 months apart. That rhythm shapes how they schedule work with every client, TVC included.

Ananas has been TVC's permaculture design partner since the early days: they ran an ideation workshop at the farm in January 2019, and delivered TVC's first GAIA map that May — the land-use plan that shaped water systems, planting zones, and future homesites.
