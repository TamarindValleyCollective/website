A Rocha India is the Indian arm of an international conservation network, with its national office and field study centre in Bangalore. Since 2003 it has worked the Bannerghatta–Hosur Landscape — the same corridor TVC sits within — running the region's longest ecological study of Asian elephants alongside habitat monitoring, afforestation, and community education.
