WeCommunities develops sustainable residential farm communities across India — pairing eco-conscious housing with regenerative agriculture on large tracts of land, run on community-first decision-making. The model has grown into multiple collectives, including Neemli Hillside near Gurgaon and Madakkal Ridges near Bangalore.
