WeCommunities develops sustainable residential farm communities across India — pairing eco-conscious housing with regenerative agriculture on large tracts of land, run on community-first decision-making about land stewardship and shared living. Members choose from vetted housing partners to customize their own homes rather than buying into a pre-built development, and take an active hand in decisions about the land itself.

Since TVC, the model has grown into further collectives: Neemli Hillside near Gurgaon (100+ bigha of agroforestry within 90 minutes of Delhi) and Madakkal Ridges in Bangalore (70+ acres of agroforestry, food forest, and animal husbandry across rolling hills), alongside a third, Rose Valley Collective. All run on chemical-free production, seasonal crops, and farm-to-table produce.

TVC's founding group partnered with WeCommunities to turn a seed idea into an actual piece of land back in 2016 — TVC was their very first project, and the model that later grew into their current collectives.
