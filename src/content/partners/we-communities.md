WeCommunities develops sustainable residential farm communities across India — pairing eco-conscious housing with regenerative agriculture on large tracts of land, run on community-first decision-making about land stewardship and shared living. Before laying out a new site, the team studies "the laws of the land" to shape a farm design plan around agroforestry, ecological design, and natural building materials, aiming for homes that minimize ecological impact while running on renewable energy and locally sourced materials.

The model has since grown into multiple collectives: Neemli Hillside Collective, a 100+ bigha agroforestry farm in the Aravalli range about 90 minutes from Gurgaon; Madakkal Ridges, a 70+ acre agroforestry and food forest collective near Bangalore; and Rose Valley Collective, also near Bangalore, with further sites planned. Member communities produce chemical-free vegetables and value-added products like pickles, oils, and dairy, and host farm visits and overnight stays of their own.

TVC's founding group partnered with WeCommunities to turn a seed idea into an actual piece of land back in 2016 — TVC was their very first project, and the model that later grew into their current collectives.
