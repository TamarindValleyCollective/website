Join **[Local Narratives](/people/partners#local-narratives)** for a day at Tamarind Valley Collective — hikes, farm meals, and a raw cooking workshop, hosted by TVC's own [Madhavan](/people/staff#madhavan).

Your local host, **[Madhavan](/people/staff#madhavan)**, moved out of Bangalore to focus on sustainability, farming, and forest regeneration, and his work is part of what's turned this land into the thriving food forest TVC is today. His deep connection with the local community and knowledge of traditional practices make him the guide for this forest and village experience.
