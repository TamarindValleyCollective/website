Slow down, walk through the land and discover the edible treasures growing all around us. From familiar plants to seasonal wild greens and other edibles, every discovery brings with it a story.

The joy of foraging truly comes alive in the kitchen. We bring back what we gather, prepare and cook together, and finally sit down to enjoy a meal made from the land's bounty.

More than just a foraging workshop, it's an opportunity to reconnect with the land, its seasons and communities, and the simple joy of good food and good company.

**[Foraging Trails and Tales](/people/partners#foraging-trails-and-tales)** is back for another edition at Tamarind Valley Collective on 28–29 November, with two ways to join: a day visit, or an overnight stay. The foraging walk is led by **[Subhashini](https://www.instagram.com/greensofkanakapura/) and [Archana](https://www.instagram.com/mylittlethoughtslane/)**, and the Sunday farm tour by TVC's own [Madhavan](/people/staff#madhavan).

## Schedule

### Day visit — Saturday 28 November

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 9:00 AM | Arrival and breakfast |
| 10:30 AM – 12:30 PM | Guided foraging walk and gathering |
| 12:30 – 2:30 PM | Preparing, cooking and sharing a foraged lunch |
| 4:00 PM | Evening snacks, followed by departure |

### Overnight experience — 28–29 November

Everything in the day visit, then stay back for a more relaxed weekend:

| When | Activity |
|---|---|
| Saturday evening | Temple trek |
| Saturday night | Bonfire and dinner |
| Saturday night | Optional night walk / herping session, depending on weather and group interest |
| Sunday morning | Breakfast, then a guided farm tour with Madhavan — the story of Tamarind Valley Collective and its journey of revival through permaculture, bringing life back to this once-degraded 100-acre landscape |

## What's included

- All meals and activities, for both the day visit and the overnight stay
- Guided foraging walk and gathering
- Cooking and sharing a foraged lunch together
- Overnight: temple trek, bonfire and dinner, optional night walk, Sunday breakfast and farm tour
