A 1 night/2 day event introducing the idea of Tamarind Valley Collective — why we chose permaculture as a design principle, what went into creating the design, the progress so far, and how well it's worked.

## Itinerary

**Day 1**

* 09:00 — Arrive at the farm, introductions
* 10:00 — Introduction to the TVC community
* 11:00 — Topology of the land, master plan view
* 12:00 — Walk the land to understand the topology
* 13:30 — Lunch
* 14:30 — Principles activity
* 16:30 — Live fence planting
* 18:00 — Retire for the day
* 19:00 — Story sharing, bonfire, dinner, retire for the night

**Day 2**

* 06:00 — Wake up and refresh
* 07:00 — Bird watching around the farm
* 09:00 — Breakfast
* 10:00 — Water management
* 12:00 — Present your plan
* 13:30 — Lunch
* 14:30 — Q&A session and closure
* 16:00 — Depart from the site

### Pricing

INR 3,600 per participant, including food and tented accommodation for the night — twin sharing in a mix of 2 bamboo huts, 3 army tents, and Coleman dome tents.

Hosted with our hospitality partner Linger.
