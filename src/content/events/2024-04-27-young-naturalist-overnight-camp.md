An introduction for children to the abundance of nature — a night outdoors in tents, a guided farm walk, a hands-on cooking workshop and art activity, storytelling, star gazing, and journaling their experience. For parents and kids aged 6–10 years, limited spots (up to 10 families).

Pricing: INR 2,500 (adult), INR 1,500 (child).

#### Facilitators

The event was conducted by Ranjini ([@leftofwrite](https://www.instagram.com/leftofwrite/)) — an author, teacher, and food connoisseur — and Madhavan, our resident naturalist, who knows the trees, birds, butterflies, and reptiles at TVC like the back of his hand.
