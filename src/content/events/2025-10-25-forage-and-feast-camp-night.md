Tamarind Valley Collective (TVC) is an experiment to see if we can live off the land — one of our ethos has been not to grow what you want to eat, but eat what grows naturally. Foraging is an integral part of that process. This camp night was organized to understand what the land has to offer.

## Schedule

| 11:00 | Introduction to the farm and self-exploration — Madhav |

| 14:00 | Forest ecosystem and foraging food — Shubhasini & Archana, Foraging Trails and Tales |
| 15:30 | Tea/coffee |

| 20:00 | Dinner and bonfire |
| 06:00 | Wake up, morning tea |
| 07:00 | Bird watching walk for enthusiasts — Madhav |
| 09:00 | Breakfast |
| 10:00 | Wrap and leave |
