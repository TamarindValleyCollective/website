- **2026-07-30** Find and fix the actual cause of the pinch-zoom black gap (Sharath) — after three
  failed/reverted attempts to fix the *symptom* (below), testing an artificial long English word
  in the homepage heading reproduced the same bug, ruling out anything Kannada-specific; traced it
  to a real bug: `.hero`/`.hero--image` are `display:flex` and their `.hero__content` child never
  got `min-width:0`, so as a flex item it couldn't shrink below its content's intrinsic
  min-content width — a long unbroken word inflated that regardless of the heading's own
  `overflow-wrap:break-word` (which only controls visual line-wrapping, not flex intrinsic
  sizing), causing genuine ~10-25px horizontal overflow that's invisible at 100% zoom but exposed
  as the black gap once mobile pinch-zoom reveals the true (too-wide) document width. Confirmed
  via `scrollWidth` before/after on both the real Kannada heading and the English test word;
  verified fixed on a real device before merging. `571072f`
